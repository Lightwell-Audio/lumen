## Audio clip editing

- **Trim, fade and shape a clip in its Clip tab.** Drag the clip's grips,
  fades and gain line inside the audio editor, slide the audio within the
  clip, or do all of it from the keyboard. See
  [Trim and fade inside an audio clip](@/guides/clips/trim-and-fade-inside-an-audio-clip.md).
- **See the whole file behind a clip.** An opened audio clip shows its whole
  file, with the part it plays bright, and zooms down to single samples. See
  [Look inside an audio clip](@/guides/clips/look-inside-an-audio-clip.md).
