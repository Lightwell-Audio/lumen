# Audio clip editing and warp: manual tests

## MT-CLIP-1 — The audio editor's canvas and the shared header (S1)

**Agent run, 2026-11-07 (test audio device, isolated settings; not the Focusrite, nothing heard).** The owner confirmed by hand that a real double-click on a clip opens the clip
editor (a synthetic double-click from the test harness could not show it, so
the runs opened the clip with Enter, the Open clip command). Passed by
looking: steps 1 to 3 (a 60 s file split at bars so the clip plays a middle
part; the Clip tab shows properties, wave and ruler; Whole file shows the
played part bright and the rest grey); step 4 Ctrl+wheel over the wave zooms
the editor and the arrangement stays put; step 5 about 40 Ctrl+wheel notches
reach the samples as dots; step 6 a click on the ruler and Space move the
beam; step 7 the MIDI editor has the same header with Pointer and Draw; step 8
light theme at 150% shows no overlap. **Step 10 passed in a second run that put the pointer over the wave:** Z zooms
the editor to the clip, X goes back to the whole file, and the arrangement above
does not move (the first run sent the keys with the pointer elsewhere, where they
zoom the arrangement as designed). **Not run:** step 9 (smallest window with the
browser open at 200%), 200% in step 8, stutter, and the colour of the bar over
the ruler.

**State:** Not run. Branch: `m9.5-s1-editor-canvas`. About ten minutes, with
headphones. Build and start from the repository root in PowerShell:

```powershell
cargo build --release -p lumen-plugin-host
```

```powershell
cargo run --release -p lumen-app
```

1. Drag a long audio file (a minute or more) onto the arrangement. Trim the
   clip's start and end so it plays a few bars from the middle.
2. Double-click the clip. **Expect:** the Clip tab shows the properties at the
   left and, beside them, the header (colour bar, name, "N bars · m:ss.s
   file", Grid, then Zoom to clip and Whole file at the right), a ruler of bar
   numbers over the file's time, and the wave of the part the clip plays,
   filling the width.
3. Click **Whole file**. **Expect:** the whole file shows; the part the clip
   plays is bright in the track colour, the rest grey on a darker wash. The
   ruler's bottom edge has a blue band over the clip.
4. Ctrl+wheel over the wave, then the wheel. **Expect:** it zooms around the
   pointer and scrolls; the arrangement above does not move.
5. Keep zooming in (Ctrl+wheel or +). **Expect:** the wave becomes a line
   through the samples, then dots on each sample. Nothing stutters.
6. Click the ruler at a bar. **Expect:** the playhead goes there. Press Space.
   **Expect:** the playhead's beam moves through the editor and the view
   pages with it. Scroll the editor with the wheel while it plays.
   **Expect:** the view stays where you put it.
7. Open a MIDI clip (or Create MIDI clip). **Expect:** its header has the
   same title, grid chip and right-hand group in the same places, with
   Pointer and Draw after the name (the audio editor gets them in S2).
   Pointer chosen is grey; Draw chosen is blue. The B key is not written in
   the control; hovering Draw shows it in the tooltip.
8. Switch to the light theme and to 150% and 200% scale (Preferences). Repeat
   2 and 3. **Expect:** nothing overlaps or is cut.
9. Make the window as small as it goes, with the browser open, at 200%.
   **Expect:** the editor's buttons read "Clip" and "File"; the name and Grid
   are shortened, never drawn over the buttons.
10. Point at the wave and press Z, then X. **Expect:** Z zooms the editor to
    the clip, X goes back; the arrangement does not move.

## MT-CLIP-2 — The audio editor's handles (S2a)

**Agent run, 2026-11-07 (test audio device, isolated settings; not the Focusrite, nothing heard).** Seen on the same machine: step 1 the grips, corner handles and the info line
("Drag a grip to trim, a corner to fade, the line for gain") on a trimmed clip; step
2 dragging the end grip moves the clip's end on the arrangement to a 1/16 grid
position, and Ctrl+Z takes the one drag back ("Undo: Trim clip"; the Alt drag was
not tried); step 3 dragging the fade-in corner makes a 1.66 s fade (the Fade in
field follows) and a double-click on its handle removes it (the S-curve menu and
the sound were not checked). **Not run:** the gain line (it was not found with
the pointer, so step 4 is open), steps 5 to 11.

**Second agent run, 2026-11-07 (same machine and test device; nothing heard).** Passed by
looking: step 4 the gain line is the thin line across the wave (about 10 px above the
wave's centre at the default panel height); dragging it down 29 px set Gain to
-14.2 dB, the wave shrank, and a double-click on it set 0.0 dB (the value shown while
dragging was not captured, only the field after); step 5, in part: with the clip
trimmed to two bars and Whole file chosen, dragging the grey part sideways moved the
audio under the clip (the ruler's file time at the clip's start changed from 0:00.0 to
0:01.0 while the clip stayed where it was on the arrangement; the test file is a repeating
pulse, so the wave looks the same, and only the ruler shows it) and Ctrl+Z took it back;
step 9, in part: Lock from the wave's right-click menu puts a padlock on the clip, greys Gain
and the fades with "Locked: unlock it to change gain and fades", and dragging the end grip
or the gain line changes nothing and the toast says "This clip is locked, so it can't be
changed. Unlock it from its right-click menu first" (the frozen-track message was not
tried). **Step 8 not confirmed:** with a clip open, the first F6 put a ring round the
detail panel (it moved on with later F6 presses), but six Tab presses after it showed no
ring on any handle in the screenshots; a synthetic key may not reach the panel as a real
one does (the same as in MT-EDIT-7 step 4), so please repeat it by hand. **Not run:**
step 5's Ctrl+Alt drag and Ctrl+Alt+Left and Right, steps 6 and 7, 10 (smallest window)
and 11 (light theme), and the sound of step 3's fades.

**Third agent run, 2026-11-07 (same machine and test device; nothing heard).** Passed by
looking: step 6, in the way the info line promises: a 5 s clip split at the playhead, the left half
opened, its fade out handle dragged left gave a 659 ms fade, and dragging the same handle on past
the clip's end made a crossfade into the right half (the X shows on the arrangement and both
curves cross in the editor; not heard). Step 7: pressing the end
grip and holding still for 3 s left the view where it was; dragging on to the window's right edge
scrolled the view along (past bar 8) and the clip grew; releasing and one Ctrl+Z took the
whole drag back (the header says "2 bars" again and the arrangement's clip is as before; the
editor's view stayed at bars 7 and 8 after the undo, and "Zoom to clip" brings it back; Follow pausing was not
checked). Step 11: in the light theme the grips, corner handles, gain line and fade curves of a
clip with a crossfade are easy to see against the white wave. Step 10, in part: at 100% in a
700 x 500 window the header shows only icons (the Grid chip reads "1/32", the name is cut
with an ellipsis) and the properties column is folded away; at 200% in 1100 x 700 the same, with
Whole file and Zoom to clip as icons and a ⋯ button, and nothing overlaps. A window forced
smaller than that (760 x 540 at 200%) cuts the transport bar and the editor's header at the right
edge, but the harness resized the window, which may go below the size a person can drag
it to, so the smallest size the window allows needs a hand check. At 150% in a 1000 x 640 window the header was
icons and a ⋯ button and nothing overlapped, either. **Step 5's Ctrl+Alt not confirmed:** with the
file shown whole, Ctrl+Alt+Right twice and a Ctrl+Alt drag on the bright part (synthetic keys and
mouse) changed nothing on the ruler, and the next Ctrl+Z undid the crossfade of step 6, so the
Ctrl+Alt gestures made no undo step; a synthetic Ctrl+Alt may not reach the app as a real one does, so
please repeat this by hand. **Still not run:** step 8's focus ring (see above) and the sound of every fade.

**State:** Not run. Branch: `m9.5-s2a-editor-handles`. About fifteen
minutes, with headphones. Build and start from the repository root in
PowerShell:

```powershell
cargo build --release -p lumen-plugin-host
```

```powershell
cargo run --release -p lumen-app
```

1. Drag an audio file of a few bars onto the arrangement, trim its start and
   end a little, and double-click it. **Expect:** in the Clip tab a grip at
   each edge of the bright part, a round handle at each top corner and a thin
   line across the wave, all quiet, and lit as the pointer passes over them.
   The info line at the bottom says what each one does.
2. Drag the end grip a beat to the left, then hold Alt and drag it a little.
   **Expect:** the first lands on the grid, the second where you let go. The
   clip on the arrangement changes with it. Ctrl+Z twice takes back one drag
   each.
3. Drag the fade in handle inwards, then its middle dot up. Right-click the
   fade and pick S-curve. Double-click the handle. **Expect:** the fade
   grows, bends, changes shape and is removed; play it each time and hear
   the fade. Each is one undo step.
4. Drag the gain line down by about 6 dB. **Expect:** the wave shrinks and
   the clip is quieter; the value in dB shows while dragging.
   Double-click the line: back to 0 dB.
5. Click **Whole file** and drag the grey part of the file sideways. Then
   hold Ctrl+Alt and drag the bright part. **Expect:** the audio slides
   under the clip and the clip stays where it is on the arrangement. With
   the pointer over the editor, Ctrl+Alt+Left and Right slide it by a grid
   step.
6. Split the clip on the arrangement with Ctrl+E, open the left half, and
   drag its fade out handle well past its end. **Expect:** a crossfade with
   the right half, heard without a click, the same as made on the
   arrangement.
7. Click **Zoom to clip**, press the end grip near the editor's right side
   and hold still. **Expect:** the view stays put. Then drag on past the
   side. **Expect:** the view scrolls along, faster the further out, and
   Follow pauses; let go and Ctrl+Z takes the whole drag back.
8. Press F6 until the detail panel has the keyboard, then Tab. **Expect:**
   the ring moves through trim start, fade in, gain, fade out and trim end.
   On the fade in, Alt+Right lengthens it and no clip moves; Delete removes
   it and the clip stays; Enter opens a box, type "250 ms" and Enter:
   **Expect:** the fade is 250 ms and the handle still has the ring.
9. Right-click the wave and choose **Lock**. Try to drag a grip. **Expect:**
   nothing moves and a message says the clip is locked. Unlock it, freeze
   the track and try again: the message names the frozen track.
10. Make the window as small as it goes with the browser open, at 100%,
    150% and 200% (Preferences). **Expect:** the header's buttons lose
    their words, then move into the ⋯ menu; nothing overlaps; at 200% the
    properties column folds and Clip properties is in the ⋯ menu.
11. Switch to the light theme and repeat 1. **Expect:** the handles are as
    easy to see.
