# Milestone 4.5 — Polish pass: plan

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-10 by the owner: every proposal of the
interface audit (`docs/audits/2026-10-10-design.md`), chosen from the
mock-ups, built before M5 so the piano roll inherits the new widgets, and
recorded as a mid milestone when finished.

**Goal:** fix the audit's bugs, make the shared widgets and sizes
consistent, and add the motion DESIGN.md promises, without changing what
is saved or heard.

**Exit criterion:** every audit finding built or explicitly deferred; each
slice's manual test passed; idle repaints and frame times unchanged; no
format change.

## 1. Rules for this milestone

- **No format change.** Everything here is look, feel and settings-free
  behaviour. If a slice finds it needs one, it stops and asks.
- **Shared widgets first.** Slice 2's switch, segmented control, slider
  handle and size tokens are what slice 3 and M5 use.
- **Mock-ups were the pitch.** Visual shifts beyond the mock-ups (1–2 pt
  moves from the size scale) are shown as before and after screenshots in
  the slice's manual test.
- **Motion costs nothing at rest.** Every new animation requests frames
  only while it runs; idle repaints are measured after slice 5 against the
  M4 figure (3 repaints in 3 s on the stress project).

## 2. Slices

Every slice ends with E2E scenarios for what it delivers, the smoke tests,
and a manual test (MT-POL-*).

### Slice 1 — Bugs
- **T1:** `widgets::value_field` takes `theme::controls(selected)` for its
  fill and hover, so it reads on `header`, `raised` and `selected_row`.
- **T2:** a `well` token (dark `#141619`, light `#D6DAE0`) for fader
  grooves and meter wells; DESIGN.md §3; a contrast test against `header`
  and `raised` in both themes.
- **U1:** the vertical scroll is clamped to the content every frame; E2E:
  a project saved scrolled past its content opens with its first track at
  the top.
- **Shots harness:** a window tall enough for the mixer, and one thread
  (the theme is process-global), in the documented command; all shots
  pass.

### Slice 2 — Shared widgets and sizes
- **S4:** `space::XXS 2 · L 16 · XL 24`; `size::CONTROL 24 ·
  CONTROL_SMALL 20 · KNOB 24 · KNOB_SMALL 18 · LABEL_COLUMN`; module
  constants move onto them (header M, S and arm to 20; knobs to two sizes;
  gaps to 2, 4 and 8); `interact_size` and `item_spacing` on the grid;
  DESIGN.md §5 matches the code.
- **S2 (the owner's C):** the handle is a solid line; within 8 pt of the
  value's box its middle opens, smoothly with the distance, to the top and
  bottom ticks (0–4 pt and 13–16 pt) once it is over the text. The same
  for pan around its value. No animation of its own.
- **S3:** scale marks placed by priority (0, −48, +6, −12, −24, −6); a mark
  within 11 pt of a placed one becomes a 3 pt tick.
- **T3:** `widgets::switch` (the knob glides over `GLIDE`, the accent when
  on) and `widgets::segmented` (a sliding thumb) replace every egui
  checkbox and radio; long lists stay menus. Accessible as a checkbox and
  as radio buttons.

### Slice 3 — Chrome
- **T4:** the detail panel's tabs as words with a 2 pt accent underline
  that slides (`GLIDE`); unbuilt tabs in `text_disabled` with their
  tooltip; the transport's toggles keep their control fill when off.
- **T5:** the arm button at rest is grey (control disc, `text_hint` dot),
  red on hover (`FAST`); armed unchanged.
- **S5:** `widgets::empty_state(icon, title, hint, action)` for the lanes
  and the browser (which names its milestone); the mixer's ghost strip
  adds a track.
- **S6:** swatches in two rows of six with a "Track colour" pill; the path
  shortened in the middle.
- **S7:** the time signature as one field (drag or wheel the numerator,
  Alt+wheel the denominator, type "7/8"); named "Time signature".

### Slice 4 — Numbers in Martian Mono
- **S1:** Martian Mono (SIL OFL) replaces Hack for monospace text, set at
  width 87.5 with a `wdth` coordinate as `fonts.rs` sets weight. The
  download is asked for first; the licence goes in `assets/fonts` and
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Widths of the position, tempo and readouts
  checked at 100% to 200% and the 960 × 540 minimum.

### Slice 5 — Motion
- **M1:** clips are drawn at a glided x: the snap correction on release
  and moves by undo, redo and commands glide (`GLIDE`); a drag follows the
  pointer exactly; the audio changes at once.
- **M4:** after undo or redo, the clips and tracks the step touched get an
  accent wash fading over 600 ms (ease-out); with Reduce motion it shows
  for the note's length without fading.
- **M2:** a folding group's tracks glide to no height (lanes and mixer
  strips), kept in the rows while they move.
- **M3:** new rows and strips grow in, deleted ones shrink out, reordered
  ones glide to their place.
- **U2:** an overlay scroll bar (6 pt, `text_secondary` at 55%) fades in
  while scrolling or near the edge and out 0.8 s later; draggable; strips
  cut at an edge fade into the ground.
- **M5:** the palette's, menus' and shortcut list's highlight slides
  (`FAST`, spring); dialogs fade out on close (`BASE`, ease-in); lit M, S
  and loop states cross-fade (`FAST`).
- DESIGN.md §6 lists what animates now; idle repaints and frame times
  measured on the stress project.

### Slice 6 — Close
- E2E `access` and the walkthrough re-run; the `lumen-design` checklist
  over every changed view at 100%, 150% and 200% in both themes.
- The milestone report in PROGRESS.md; CLAUDE.md §12 updated; then M5 is
  proposed.

## 3. Risks

| ID | Risk | Addressed by |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The size scale shifts layouts the owner already approved. | Before and after screenshots per view in MT-POL-2; anything beyond 2 pt asked first |
| R2 | Animations keep the app repainting. | Each uses `motion::glide` or `fade`, which stop requesting frames when settled; idle repaints measured in slice 5 |
| R3 | Glided positions break hit-testing (a click lands where the clip is going, not where it is drawn). | Hit-testing uses the drawn position while a glide runs; E2E clicks during a glide |
| R4 | Martian Mono is too wide for the transport at 960 × 540. | Width 87.5, checked at the minimum window; Geist Mono as the fallback choice |
