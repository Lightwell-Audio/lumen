# Milestone 2 — Audio Engine, Devices & Transport: proposed plan

**Status:** Approved and in progress (started 2026-10-06).

## Slices
1. **`lumen-audio` device layer:** native ASIO backend from P-ASIO, with
   channel selection, driver-reset handling, and **device-loss recovery**
   (keep the session, show the lost state, reconnect when the device returns;
   found by MT-ASIO-5).
2. **WASAPI backend** (shared; exclusive as a should-have). macOS and Linux
   through cpal behind the same interface; compiled and unit-tested in CI, no
   hardware testing yet.
3. **`lumen-engine`** grown from P-ENGINE: transport (play, stop, pause,
   loop, seek), tempo map, sample-accurate timing. RealtimeSanitizer on Linux
   CI.
4. **Transport bar with vector icons** (owner request): play, pause, stop,
   record, loop and metronome as crisp vector icons (Vello paths), with
   tooltips and shortcuts.
5. **Click-to-locate** on the ruler (first part of the owner's
   Ableton-style scrubbing request; the scrub strip, audible scrubbing and
   ruler drag-zoom follow in M3).
6. **Audio file playback** on a simple timeline. Decoder: **symphonia**
   (MPL-2.0, owner decision 2026-10-06): used unmodified, licence notice
   shipped, recorded as a cargo-deny exception.
7. **Metronome, test tone, CPU and dropout meters, Audio On/Off, panic.**
8. **Device selector** in the app: driver, device, rate, buffer, latencies,
   ASIO control panel.
