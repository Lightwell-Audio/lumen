# Milestone 8 — Drum maps, drum editor and built-in devices: plan

**Status.** Planned on 2026-11-12 on branch `m8-plan`. Waits for the owner's
choices (§2) and the approval of 9.5. Nothing is built.

The owner put M8 after M9 (2026-10-23) and after 9.5 (2026-11-03). This plan is
written to be built by **delegated agents, one slice per agent**: every slice
in §5 is a self-contained brief in the form of
`.claude/skills/references/delegation.md`, with the files it owns, the
interfaces it may rely on, its acceptance checks and its effort. The parent
session cuts the branches, hands out the briefs, reviews and merges (§4).

**Goal.** A MIDI track can carry a drum map, and its clips open in a drum
editor and a step sequencer that show sounds, not keys. Lumen ships the
built-in essentials, so a song can be made with no plugin at all, and every
built-in parameter is automatable, mappable and sample-exact like a plugin's.

**Exit criterion** (the drum and device part of CLAUDE.md §10's walkthrough).
Someone can:

- make an instrument track with the built-in sampler, drop their own one-shots
  on its pads and attach the 808-style map;
- program a pattern in the step sequencer, then refine it in the drum editor
  (velocity, one lane quantized, the hi-hats choking each other);
- send the kick and snare to their own mixer tracks and group them;
- add an EQ, a compressor keyed from the kick, a delay and a reverb, all
  built-in, and automate the EQ's band frequency;
- export, and hear in the file exactly what played.

**Requirements covered** (status in `docs/PROGRESS.md`):

- §6.4 drum maps, every [M] row; the drum editor and the step sequencer [M];
- §6.8 the built-in essentials [M] (utility, parametric EQ with analyzer,
  compressor with sidechain, limiter, delay, reverb, the synth's controls,
  the sampler); spectrum analyzer and tuner [S];
- §6.5 "everything that has a value can be automated" and "sample-accurate for
  built-in devices" [M], which wait on the built-ins;
- §6.3 MIDI effects [S] and §6.6 slice audio to MIDI or a drum map [S], behind
  the cut line (§3);
- the content faces and chord names moved here from M5 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`,
  owner table, 2026-10-11), behind the cut line.

**Not in M8:** the legacy drum-map format import [L]; Euclidean and random fill
[L]; device racks with macros [L]; bundled sample libraries (out of scope for
1.0, CLAUDE.md §8: the sampler starts empty and says how to fill it);
convolution reverb; linear-phase EQ.

So the drum half is mostly **model, translation and editors** on top of the
MIDI path, and the device half is **one in-process effect path** in the engine
that every built-in then plugs into, plus DSP and faces.

### DM: where a drum map lives

- **A (rec).** In the project's map list. A track names a map by id, so two
  kit tracks share one map and an edit to it applies to both. "Make unique"
  copies it for one track. Presets and user maps are copied into the project
  when attached, so a project never depends on the user's files.
- **B.** A copy on every track. Simpler, but a fix to a map must be made once
  per track.

### DV: the drum editor and the step sequencer (specimens)

- **A (rec).** One clip editor with a two-way switch in the shared header
  (`app/clip/clip_header.rs`): **Lanes** (the drum editor) and **Steps**
  (the step sequencer), both of the same clip. A clip on a track with a map
  opens in Lanes; the switch also offers **Keys** (the piano roll with drum
  names), so all three views of one clip are one click apart.
- **B.** Lanes only in the Clip tab; the step sequencer as a face on the
  arrangement's clip, edited in place.
- **C.** Two separate tabs in the detail panel.

### DE: what a drum hit looks like in the lanes

- **A (rec).** A diamond at the hit's start, its fill the velocity; length is
  hidden unless the sound has no length override and the lane is set to
  "Show length".
- **B.** A short bar with its length, as in the piano roll.

### SQ: the step sequencer's grid (specimens)

- **A (rec).** One row per visible lane, 16 steps by default (8 to 64, any
  resolution of the grid list, triplets included), a page strip when the clip
  is longer than one page, velocity as the fill height, accent as a rim. A
  click toggles, a vertical drag sets velocity, Shift+click toggles accent.
- **B.** As A, with per-step velocity shown in a lane under the grid instead
  of in the cell.

### RT: per-sound routing (§6.4 "the instrument or a specific instrument output")

- **A (rec).** Each sound's **Output** is *Main* or one of the instrument's
  output pairs. The built-in sampler honours it per pad. A VST3 instrument
  decides its own outputs inside the plugin, so for VST3 the column offers
  only the pairs the plugin has and says "set in the plugin" when the plugin
  cannot be told; picking a pair makes (or reuses) the audio track that plays
  it (the existing `plugin_output` tracks), named after the sound. A sound can
  also be sent to **another instrument track** (layering a separate kick).
- **B.** A only for the sampler; VST3 routing stays in the plugin.

### SP: the sampler's shape

- **A (rec).** One device with three faces chosen at its top: **One shot**
  (one sample, start, end, loop, ADSR, filter), **Zones** (key and velocity
  zones from many samples) and **Pads** (one pad per sound of the track's
  drum map; the pad's name, colour and output come from the map).
- **B.** Two devices: a melodic sampler (One shot and Zones) and a drum
  sampler (Pads).

### BF: built-in device faces (specimens)

- **A.** The generic eight-knob strip for every built-in, as for plugins.
- **B.** A drawn face for every built-in.
- **C (rec).** A drawn face where a picture is the control (EQ curve with
  the analyzer, compressor and limiter transfer and gain-reduction meter,
  the sampler's wave and pads), the strip for the rest (utility, delay,
  reverb, synth, tuner). Every face keeps the card's chrome, bypass and
  preset menu, and every parameter is still in All parameters.

### PR: built-in presets

- **A (rec).** Built-ins load and save the same preset files as the chain's
  preset menu uses for plugins, in Lumen's own preset folder, with a few
  factory presets per device (made from parameter values, no samples).
- **B.** Factory presets only in M8.

### CO: choke groups

- **A (rec).** Enforced by Lumen, before the instrument: a new hit of a sound
  in a choke group ends the sounding notes of the others (a note-off at the
  same sample), for clip notes and live input alike. Works with any
  instrument. The sampler also fades its choked voice in 5 ms so it never
  clicks.
- **B.** Only the sampler chokes.

### CL: the cut line

- **A (rec).** M8 ships when slices P to D4, B0 to B6 and Z are done. The
  [S] work (X1 MIDI effects, X2 spectrum analyzer and tuner, X3 slice audio
  to pads, X4 content faces and chord names, X5 the lit kit) is built after Z only if the
  owner says so; otherwise it moves to M10 or after 1.0.
- **B.** All of it in M8.

## 4. How the parent runs the agents

- **One agent per slice, one branch per slice.** The parent cuts the branch,
  hands the brief from §5 unchanged plus the current `CLAUDE.md` §12, and
  owns integration. An agent never merges, pushes to another slice's branch,
  or starts its own agents (delegation.md).
- **Model and effort** as in delegation.md and the Effort column. High slices
  (D0, D2, D4, B0, B2, B5, Z) are reviewed by an independent reviewer agent
  before merge; Medium and Low slices by the parent.
- **Hot spots** that two lanes touch. The parent resolves them at merge time
  rather than giving them to one agent:
  - `commands/mod.rs` and `commands::ALL`: new commands go at the end, so two
    branches conflict only by order; the later merge moves its block after
    the earlier one and moves the order test's last variant.
  - The format version: whichever of D0, B0, B4, B5, D4 merges first takes the
    next number; the later one renumbers its migration and golden fixture
    before merge. An agent writes its migration as one function named for its
    content (`add_drum_maps`), so renumbering is one line and a file name.
  - `history::unchanged`, `site/docs/data/undocumented.txt`,
    `docs/DECISIONS.md` (ADR numbers are assigned by the parent at merge;
    agents write `ADR-XXXX` and the parent fills it in), `docs/PROGRESS.md`
    and `CLAUDE.md` §12 (the parent writes these two).
- **Every brief carries the same standing rules** (not repeated in §5):
  - load `lumen-architecture` before code and `lumen-design` before UI;
  - follow the gotchas in `CLAUDE.md` §12 (commands at the end of `ALL`,
    new persisted fields bump the format and join `history::unchanged`,
    `App::typing` for keys, draw only what is in view, notifications in full,
    menus list what can apply, E2E waits with `lumen_testkit` and
    `Lumen::wait_for`, never counted frames);
  - every edit a named `Document` method, one undo step; every action a
    command in the palette with a tooltip and context-menu entry; UI values
    from `design/` tokens;
  - the audio thread allocates nothing, locks nothing and never blocks
    (allocation-guard test for every new real-time path);
  - before handing back: `cargo fmt`, workspace clippy with `-D warnings`,
    the crate tests, `cargo test -p lumen-app --test e2e` and the smoke
    tests; say what was not run (WSL perf gate, macOS cross-clippy, shots at
    three scales) rather than claiming it;
  - a new user-facing action with no docs page is tagged `@m8` in
    `undocumented.txt` (ADR-0133); guides are written in Z.
  - product-neutral naming (`tests/third_party_names.rs`).
- **Handoff** from every agent: the branch's commits, what was built against
  the acceptance list item by item, checks run with results, deviations with
  reasons, the ADR draft, the MT-* script, and open risks. The parent verifies
  by running the checks itself; an agent's report is not verification.

### D1 — Map editor and drum names

- **Objective.** A user can see and edit the map, and the piano roll reads as
  drums when a map is attached.
- **Inputs.** D0's model and edits; `note_editor/` (key column in
  `paint.rs`, `glyphs.rs`); `widgets/`, `design/`; `lumen-design`.
- **Scope (owns).** The fifteen drum icons, drawn as vector paths beside
  the existing icons (`design/icons.rs` or a `drum_icons.rs` beside it) from
  round 3's shapes, crisp at 12 to 28 pt; new `lumen-app/src/drum_map_window/` (a floating window
  like the Mappings window, `app/mappings_window.rs`, for the table); the key
  column's names in `note_editor/`; import and export.
- **Behaviour.** A table of sounds with every D0 field editable in place
  (type-in, wheel, double-click resets), add, remove, reorder by drag, colour,
  icon (a 5 × 3 grid popover of the icons, keyboard-navigable, named);
  a learn button per row (the next note played sets the input note); preset
  chooser, Make unique, Rename, Save as user map (to Lumen's user folder,
  `.lumendrums`, JSON, versioned), Import, Export. The piano roll's key column
  shows each sound's icon and name at its input note, unmapped keys dimmed, with "Hide
  unmapped keys".
- **Acceptance.** Every edit one undo step; keyboard-only edit of a row;
  import of an exported map round-trips; a damaged file is refused with a
  message read in full; E2E for attach, edit name and input note, export and
  import; shots of the window and the key column in both themes at three
  scales.
- **Effort.** Medium.

### D2 — Drum editor (Lanes)

- **Objective.** §6.4's drum editor: one lane per sound.
- **Inputs.** D0, D1; P's chosen DV and DE; `note_editor/` for gestures,
  selection, snapping, zoom and the velocity lane (reuse, do not fork);
  `app/clip/clip_header.rs`; `quantize.rs`.
- **Scope (owns).** New `lumen-app/src/drum_editor/`; the header's view
  switch (DV A: Lanes, Steps, Keys); routing the Clip tab to it.
- **Behaviour.** Lanes in map order with the sound's icon, name, colour, audition
  (click the name), mute and solo (the map's, so the same as D1's table);
  hits drawn per DE; draw (click), erase, move, copy-drag, select by box,
  nudge, velocity in the shared velocity lane; per-lane quantize (Q applies
  the lane's own grid when the sound has one); reorder lanes by drag (reorders
  the map); "Hide unused" (persisted per clip view or per track, decided in
  the ADR). The same snapping, selection, zoom and drag rules as the piano
  roll (CLAUDE.md §5); Tab between lanes, arrows between hits, accessible
  names ("Snare, bar 2 beat 3, velocity 96").
- **Acceptance.** Every gesture one undo step; edits equal the same edit in
  the piano roll (property tests on the model); 100,000-note clip frame time
  within the piano roll's (bench); E2E per gesture group; shots.
- **Effort.** High (largest UI slice; shared gesture code).

### D3 — Step sequencer (Steps)

- **Objective.** §6.4's step view of the same clip.
- **Inputs.** D2's editor shell, SQ choice, the grid list
  (`lumen_core::grid`).
- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-app/src/drum_editor/steps/`.
- **Behaviour.** Step count 8 to 64 and resolution from the grid list; a step
  is on when a hit starts inside it (off-grid hits shown at their step with a
  mark, never moved by viewing); click toggles, drag sets velocity, Shift
  toggles accent (accent = a velocity the clip remembers, default 127,
  owner-adjustable), paging for longer clips, a playing-step light.
- **Acceptance.** Toggling on and off returns the clip to its notes exactly;
  E2E for toggle, velocity drag, accent, paging; keyboard: arrows move the
  step focus, Space toggles (in the grid only, never transport when the grid
  has focus — check `App::typing` conventions).
- **Effort.** Medium.

### D4 — Per-sound routing

- **Objective.** RT A: each sound can play out of its own output and mixer

- **Inputs.** D0's `Route`; `Track.plugin_output` and `chain/outputs.rs`;
  `hosting/outputs.rs`; B5's per-pad outputs for the sampler half; D1's
  icons.
- **Scope (owns).** Route editing in D1's table, the track creation in
  `document/drums.rs`, `midi/play.rs` for sends to another instrument track.
- **Behaviour.** Output column: Main, the instrument's pairs (named by the
  plugin or the sampler), or another instrument track; picking a pair makes
  or reuses its audio track, named after the sound, placed under the kit
  track; "Route all sounds to tracks" makes them all in one undo step and
  offers to group them. A track made for sounds shows the icon of the first
  sound routed to it in its header (in place of the colour chip; derived from
  the routes, not saved), lit while one of its sounds plays.
- **Acceptance.** Sampler pad on pair 3 heard only on its track (engine
  test); a sound sent to another track plays that track's instrument with no
  hanging notes when the route changes mid-note; E2E; MT.
- **Effort.** High.

### B0 — The built-in effect path and Utility

- **Objective.** One path by which every built-in effect runs in the audio
  thread in-process, with parameters that behave exactly as a plugin's do
  (card strip, All parameters, automation, MIDI learn, override, undo,
  presets), proven with Utility.
- **Inputs.** `lumen-devices/src/{lib.rs,instrument.rs,hosted/}`;
  `lumen-engine/src/mixer/{spec.rs,compile.rs,plan/,auto.rs,params.rs,hosts.rs}`;
  `lumen-app/src/engine/routing.rs`, `chain/` (strip, params, card),
  `hosting/parameters.rs` (how plugin parameters reach the UI);
  `lumen-project/src/model/device.rs`, `automation.rs`.
- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/effect.rs` (the trait),
  `lumen-devices/src/params.rs` (parameter tables), `lumen-devices/src/utility/`;
  `mixer::spec::Device::Builtin`; the plan's in-process run; a
  `lumen-app/src/builtin/` module that builds built-ins from the model and
  gives the chain their parameters.
- **Design (to confirm in the ADR).**
  - `trait Effect: Send { fn process(&mut self, rate, frames, io: &mut [&mut
    [f32]; 2], sidechain: Option<[&[f32]; 2]>, params: &ParamEvents); fn
    latency(&self) -> u32; fn tail(&self) -> u32; fn reset(&mut self); }`.
    Parameters arrive as (sample, id, normalised value) in the block, from
    the same lanes `ParamBlock` carries for plugins; each device smooths its
    own.
  - A static `ParamInfo` table per device: stable `u32` id, name, unit,
    range, taper, default, steps, text from value and value from text. Ids
    are never reused (a test pins each table).
  - State is `Device.params` (the existing map), so undo and save need no
    blob; a `version` per built-in lets a later build migrate its
    parameters. Whether that is a new field (format bump) or the state blob
    is the slice's call, in the ADR.
  - Built-ins are made on the UI side and handed to the audio thread as the
    hosted devices are; a parameter change from the UI is a lock-free message.
  - Denormals flushed; no allocation after construction.
- **Utility.** Gain, pan (with the track's pan law), width (0 to 200%),
  mono (with a bass-mono frequency), polarity per side, swap, DC block.
- **Acceptance.**
  - automating Utility's gain is sample-exact and the same at every block
    size (golden render), offline equals real time;
  - allocation guard and RTSan over a block with automation;
  - card strip, All parameters, MIDI learn, override and Re-enable, undo,
    presets save and load, copy between tracks — each with an E2E;
  - `engine_builtin_64` bench: 64 tracks × (Utility) recorded, perf gate
    unchanged for projects without built-ins;
  - the KNOWN_ISSUES "built-in effects have no tails" row answered by the
    trait's `tail`.
- **Effort.** High (engine, real-time, cross-crate).

### B1 — Parametric EQ with analyzer

- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/eq/`, `lumen-devices/src/analyzer.rs`
  (FFT on a lock-free snapshot ring, read by the UI; no allocation in the
  audio thread), the EQ face in `lumen-app/src/chain/faces/eq.rs`.
- **Behaviour.** 8 bands (low cut and high cut with 12 to 48 dB/oct, low and
  high shelf, bell, notch), frequency, gain, Q per band, on/off per band,
  output gain; the face draws the summed curve and the analyzer (pre or post),
  drags points (Shift fine, double-click resets, wheel on a point sets Q),
  every point a Tab stop with its accessible name. Coefficients glide without
  zipper noise under automation.
- **Acceptance.** Magnitude response within 0.1 dB of the analytic biquad at
  test frequencies; automation of a band's frequency click-free (no sample
  step above a threshold in a sweep render); golden render; face shots; E2E.
- **Effort.** Medium.

### B2 — Compressor and limiter

- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/dynamics/`, faces in
  `chain/faces/dynamics.rs`, the card's sidechain chooser reused from
  `chain/sidechain.rs` for built-ins.
- **Behaviour.** Compressor: threshold, ratio, knee, attack, release, makeup
  (auto), mix, peak or RMS detection, sidechain input with a high-pass and
  Listen, gain-reduction meter. Limiter: ceiling, release, lookahead
  (reported latency, so delay compensation lines it up), true-peak mode
  (oversampled).
- **Acceptance.** Static curve within 0.1 dB of the formula; the limiter
  never exceeds its ceiling on a test set (sample peak; true peak within
  0.2 dB in true-peak mode); a sidechain keyed from another track ducks
  sample-aligned with compensation (engine test as ADR-0041's); latency shows
  in the transport's compensation chip; E2E.
- **Effort.** High (sidechain and latency in the graph).

### B3 — Delay and reverb

- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/{delay,reverb}/`.
- **Behaviour.** Delay: time in notes (synced to the tempo map, following
  tempo changes without clicks) or ms, feedback, ping-pong, filters in the
  loop, mix, freeze. Reverb: an algorithmic stereo reverb (pre-delay, size,
  decay, damping, width, early/late mix, mix). Both report tails, so they ring
  out after Stop and in bounce and export (`tail` honoured by the render's
  automatic tail).
- **Acceptance.** A synced delay's echo lands on the beat sample-exact at
  120 and after a tempo change; reverb's decay time measured within 10% of the
  setting; deterministic (offline equals real time); tails heard after Stop
  (E2E `listen`); CPU per instance recorded.
- **Effort.** Medium.

### B4 — The synth's controls

- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/synth/`, its `ParamInfo` table, the
  migration of the stored preset name to parameter values.
- **Behaviour.** Two oscillators (shape, octave, detune, level), noise, the
  filter (type, cutoff, resonance, envelope amount, key follow), amp and
  filter ADSR, one LFO (rate synced or free, to pitch, cutoff or amp), glide,
  voices (1 to 16), velocity sensitivity. The five presets are parameter sets
  that sound as before (golden renders of the old presets, bit-identical or
  within a stated tolerance agreed in the ADR).
- **Acceptance.** Old projects open sounding as before; automating cutoff is
  sample-exact (the walkthrough's "automate a filter" works on a built-in);
  allocation guard; E2E.
- **Effort.** Medium.

### B5 — Sampler

- **Scope (owns).** `lumen-devices/src/sampler/`, sample loading in
  `lumen-app/src/builtin/samples.rs` (decoding and rate conversion through
  `lumen-media` on a worker, handed to the audio thread as shared immutable
  buffers; freed off the audio thread), its faces in `chain/faces/sampler/`,
  the model's sample references (media pool, relink, collect and save know
  them).
- **Behaviour.** SP A. One shot: start, end, loop, reverse, ADSR, filter,
  pitch, velocity to level. Zones: key and velocity ranges, root key, drop
  several files to map them by name or by order. Pads: one pad per sound of
  the track's drum map (or 16 when no map), drop a file on a pad, audition,
  per-pad level, pan, tune, output pair (RT), choke fade (CO). Missing sample
  shows the media pool's missing state with Relink.
- **Acceptance.** A sample played at its root key equals the file sample for
  sample; pitched playback within the resampler's tested error; a project
  with a sampler survives save, collect and save to another folder, reopen;
  missing samples relinked; freeing a large sample never runs on the audio
  thread (allocation guard); E2E for each face; MT with the owner's own
  one-shots.
- **Effort.** High.

### B6 — Built-ins in the browser and factory presets

- **Scope (owns).** The browser's Devices tab listing for built-ins
  (`lumen-app/src/browser/`), factory preset tables per device.
- **Behaviour.** A Lumen category per kind (Instruments, Dynamics, EQ and
  filter, Time, Utility, Analysis), search, favourites and recents as for
  plugins, drag to a track or chain, double-click adds; PR A presets.
- **Acceptance.** E2E: search "comp", double-click, card appears; drag to an
  empty area makes a track. Safe mode still loads built-ins (they are not
  plugins).
- **Effort.** Low.

### Z — Close

### Behind the cut line (CL)

- **X1 MIDI effects.** A `MidiEffect` trait (events in, events out, on the
  audio thread, bounded output) and a MIDI slot before the instrument in the
  chain's ordering rules; arpeggiator (synced), chord, scale, velocity, note
  length; hanging-note safety through the hold tracker. Format change.
- **X2 Spectrum analyzer and tuner.** Devices that pass audio through and
  draw B1's analyzer and a pitch readout (YIN or similar) in their faces.
- **X3 Slice audio to pads.** From an audio clip's transients (M9.5's

#### X5 — The lit kit (stretch; punted by the owner, 2026-11-12)

Kept as written when it was punted. The owner found the drawn kit messy
(rounds 3 and 3b): before it is built, the kit is redrawn and shown again as
specimens. It would add `DrumIcon::piece()` to D0's icons.


- **Objective.** KIT: see which piece of the kit is playing, at a glance, in
  the drum editor and on the arrangement.
- **Inputs.** D0's `DrumIcon::piece()`; D2's editor shell and header; round
  3's drawing (`docs/specimens/m8/kit.html`, `kit()`); the track header code
  (`arrangement/` headers); the engine's report of notes played (the one the
  piano roll's playing keys read if it covers a track's notes after the map;
  else X5 adds a lock-free per-track ring of played sounds, as the meters
  have, read by the UI thread).
- **Scope (owns).** New `lumen-app/src/drum_editor/kit/` (the drawing, its
  layout and hit areas, the lights' decay); the Kit button in the drum
  editor's header; the small kit in the drum track's header.
- **Behaviour.**
  - The kit is built from the map (round 3b, `docs/specimens/m8/kits.html`;
    nothing about it is saved), by a pure `kit::layout(&[Sound]) -> Kit`:
    - one kick (every Kick sound), one snare (Snare and Rim), one hi-hat
      (every hat; it opens when an Open hat plays), one ride (Ride sounds,
      bell included);
    - each Tom a rack tom over the kick (up to 4, small to large in map
      order), each Floor tom a floor tom to the right (up to 3), each Crash
      its own cymbal (up to 4: left, right, far left, far right);
    - every other sound (clap, cowbell, shaker, percussion, effect) and any
      piece past the limits is an icon pad in a tray under the kit, shown up
      to what fits, then "+n" (only used sounds when Hide unused is on); a map
      with no kit piece shows only the pads, larger and named;
    - an absent piece takes its space with it, the whole is scaled to fit,
      and on a map edit the pieces glide to their places and a new one fades
      in, outlined in the accent for a moment (Reduce motion: it changes at
      once);
    - several sounds on one piece light it in the colour of the one just
      played.
    Unit tests pin the layout of each preset and of the limits; changing a
    sound's icon is the way to shape the kit.
  - A hit lights its piece in the sound's colour, brightness by velocity,
    fading over `motion::NOTE`; muted or unsoloed sounds do not light.
    Reduce motion: lit for the hit, no fade. Repaint only while a light
    fades.
  - Kit panel: shown or hidden by a Kit button in the drum editor's header
    (kept per user); click a piece to audition its sound and pick its lane;
    Tab through the pieces, Space auditions the focused one; each piece has
    an accessible name ("Snare, plays Snare and Rim").
  - Header kit: on a drum track's header at normal height and up, the kit
    only (no tray); folds away at the smallest height; a picture only (clicks select the track as
    elsewhere on the header).
  - The lit state is never colour alone: a lit piece also gains a bright
    outline.
- **Acceptance.** Lights follow playback within one frame of the engine's
  report (E2E with `listen` and a frame of the header); no allocation in the
  report's write path (allocation guard); 200 drum tracks visible, idle CPU
  stays near zero and the header bench within the arrangement's budget;
  shots of the panel and header at three scales in both themes; MT-KIT-1 by
  eye and ear.
- **Effort.** Medium.

## 6. Decisions taken unless the owner objects

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| E1 | Built-in devices run in-process on the audio thread, never in a host process: they are Lumen's own code under the same real-time tests as the engine. Safe mode loads them |
| E2 | Built-in parameter ids are stable `u32`s, never reused; the automation target stays `Target::Device { device, param }`, so lanes, MIDI learn and overrides need no new kind |
| E3 | Drum-map translation happens on the UI side when the engine's MIDI tracks are built (`midi/play.rs`), except choke and live input, which the engine applies from a small table it is handed (no allocation) |
| E4 | A recording on a mapped track stores input notes (D0); exporting a MIDI file of a mapped track writes the **played** (output) notes, and the clip export offers "as stored" too |
| E5 | The drum editor and the step sequencer reuse the piano roll's selection, snapping, zoom and gesture code; a fork is a review finding |
| E6 | Every built-in has golden renders, an allocation-guard test, and a block-size invariance test (64, 128, 512, 1024 and an odd 97) |
| E7 | DSP is written in Lumen (permissive dependencies only, e.g. an FFT crate under MIT/Apache); no GPL code |

## 7. Validation

- **Audio:** golden renders per built-in and per drum-map feature; offline
  equals real time with built-ins and maps; block-size invariance; the
  allocation guard and RTSan over every built-in and the choke path; no
  hanging notes under 400 random commands with maps, choke and route changes
  (the existing fuzz test extended).
- **Performance:** `engine_builtin_64` (64 tracks with EQ, compressor, delay
  and reverb at 128 frames / 48 kHz, inside half the block on the reference
  machine); the perf gate unchanged for projects without built-ins; drum
  editor and step sequencer frame times with 100,000 hits.
- **Format:** each bump with a migration and a frozen golden fixture; a
  project from before M8 opens sounding the same (the synth's presets
  included).
- **E2E:** one scenario per behaviour group in every slice, then
  `walkthrough_drums`.
- **Manual (owner):** MT-DRUM-1 (D0, D1), MT-DRUM-2 (D2, D3), MT-DRUM-3 (D4),
  MT-FX-1 (B0, B6), MT-FX-2 (B1, B2), MT-FX-3 (B3, B4), MT-SAMPLER-1 (B5),
  MT-M8-1.

## 8. Open risks

1. **B0 is the hinge of the device lane.** If its trait or parameter path is
   wrong, five slices inherit it. B0 is reviewed by an independent agent and
   by the parent before B1 to B5 start, and the trait is tried against the
   compressor's sidechain and the limiter's latency on paper in its ADR.
2. **Shared gesture code (D2).** The piano roll's gestures may need lifting
   into a shared module first; if that refactor is large, D2 splits into D2a
   (lift, no behaviour change, its own tests) and D2b (the lanes).
3. **Choke with VST3 instruments** depends on the plugin honouring note-off
   at once; a plugin with long release still rings. The sampler fades; for
   plugins the MT says what to expect.
4. **Sample memory.** Large multi-sample sets in memory; Zones may need disk
   streaming (R6's reader) later. M8 loads in memory with a size warning above
   a limit recorded in the ADR.
5. **Format churn.** Up to four bumps in one milestone across parallel
   branches; the renumbering rule in §4 must be followed exactly, or a
   fixture is frozen at the wrong number.
6. **Sound quality is judged by ear.** Reverb and compressor character are
   taste; factory presets and defaults wait for the owner's listening in
   MT-FX-2 and MT-FX-3.

## 9. Alternatives rejected

- **Storing output notes and translating back for display.** Swapping a map
  would rewrite every clip, against §6.4's "never destroys the notes".
- **Running built-ins in the plugin host process** for uniformity: adds a
  process hop and its latency to Lumen's own, tested code for no isolation
  gain.
- **One combined "drum machine" device** that owns map, sequencer and sounds:
  the map would then only work with that device, against "attach to any
  instrument".
