# Interface audit: spacing, clarity and cohesion (2026-10-27)

**Scope:** every panel, popover, menu, window and interaction in
`crates/lumen-app`, with the mixer and the browser looked at most closely.
The owner asked four questions: are dividers missing anywhere; is there
enough room in the wells, and does it look professional; is the contrast
good enough; are items spaced inconsistently? A fifth question: do any parts
of the app look out of place?
**Asked by:** the owner, 2026-10-27, with one bug report: the command
palette's height changes with the description of the chosen row.
**Evidence:** 426 screenshots from the audit harness
(`tests/audit_shots`, `SHOTS=<dir> cargo test -p lumen-app --test
audit_shots -- --ignored --test-threads=1`) in three looks: dark at 100%,
light at 150% and dark at 200% (the 960 × 540 minimum window). Two scenes
were added for this audit: `browser`, with eleven pretend plugins from three
vendors and one blocked, and `mixer_full`, with a group, two returns and a
MIDI track, at a tall and a short panel. The pixel measurements were made on
crops of those shots, and the causes were confirmed in the code.
**Mock-ups:** every variant is drawn in a private artifact,
https://claude.ai/artifact/Miib3NqcoRrmZJnEfmyFDR. Nothing below is built until the owner chooses, except the
palette bug fix (P1), which is built and waits on its layout choice.

## Answers in short

- **Dividers.** The longer menus are missing them: the track menu runs
  seventeen rows with two dividers, and a strip's menu has one. The browser
  has no line between its filters and its list. Mixer strips have no
  divider of their own; a return or the master gets one only because it
  starts a new column.
- **Wells and room.** The palette, the clip card and the quantize popover
  hold their spacing well. Menu rows are 33 pt tall, 37 pt apart, against
  DESIGN.md's 24 pt; menus read as loose lists and the track menu reaches
  the bottom of the window. On a strip the gap above pan is about 45 pt
  where every other row is 24.
- **Contrast.** A category tag on a selected browser row almost vanishes:
  it does not step up as `theme::controls(selected)` asks. The greyed
  Solo/Cue switch on the master reads as broken. The loudness column's "—"
  reads as a stray line. Everything else passes the contrast test.
- **Spacing.** The browser's category chips sit 12 pt further in than the
  search field and form a ragged two-column grid. The type filter stops
  short of the search field's right edge. The fader scale's −6 and −12 sit
  about 1 pt apart.
- **Out of place.** A strip's M, S and arm buttons are three separate
  buttons, though the track header made them one well (B·1). A device row
  shows a tag and a star at rest, which cuts names short ("Bus compr…").
  The device chain's empty state is a small caption in the corner where
  every other panel has a centred title and hint. In the save question,
  "Save" is not the primary button.

## Owner's choices, round one (2026-10-27)

- **A** for P1 (built), T1, T2, W1, M2, X7, X8, B1 to B7 and D1.
- **M1:** A's spacing, but the hover highlight sits tight on the keycaps in
  every menu. The owner asked whether 32 pt is the middle ground (round two).
- **M3:** rows that cannot act are left out. A row that could act but lacks
  something stays in and says what it needs.
- **X1 to X4:** A. The M, S and arm well must not overlap, and the trim
  value needs room (round two).
- **X6:** A. The owner wants to see it in context (round two).
- **B1:** the search magnifier should be larger (round two). The segmented
  type filter is preferred.
- **X5:** open.
- The owner also asked for the track and clip menus, the info line's
  hints, the Pointer and Tempo controls, and the whole transport bar to be
  reviewed (round two).

## Kept as they are

- The keycaps' coloured lights in menus (K5, the owner's choice).
- The lane chooser in the piano roll uses pills, not underline tabs (L A,
  chosen from specimens).
- The single "Devices" tab: Samples and Projects tabs are [M] items that
  will join it.
- The full blue tint on a selected strip; the group rail across a group's
  strips.

## Not covered

The harness cannot open plugin editor windows, the shortcut editor's
rebind flow, or drag ghosts mid-drag; those were checked in the code only.
Three scene labels came up missing in the harness (the blocked row's name,
"Add track" at 200%, and a "+" that landed on a send knob); the shots around
them were taken from other states.
