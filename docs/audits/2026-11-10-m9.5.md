# M9.5 review — 2026-11-10

Branch: `m9.5-s7-close`. This is the end-of-milestone review the
`lumen-review` skill asks for, before the M9.5 report. It measures mini
milestone 9.5 (S0 to S6: the audio clip editor, the clip properties strip,
warp in Repitch, Melodic, Full mix and Rhythmic, detection and markers, pitch)
against `CLAUDE.md` §2 to §5 and §10, the architecture and design skills, and
`docs/milestones/M9.5.md`. Following the skill, nothing found here that needs
a choice is fixed until the owner says so.

## Scope and evidence

This was a sweep of M9.5's own diff (`238bbc6d...HEAD`, 113 files under
`crates/`), done by one reviewer without subagents. It is narrower than the M9
review, which read the other crates too; they were not re-read here.

- **Gates:** run on this branch. The results are in the table below.
- **Mechanical scans** over the lines M9.5 added (non-test code): `allow(`,
  `unwrap(` and `expect(`, colour literals, duration literals, `Window::new`,
  `DragValue`, `TODO`/`FIXME`, `std::fs`, `doc.apply(`, `"Lumen"` in strings.
  And a count of production lines (before the inline tests) in every source
  file over 800.
- **Read by hand:** where each `unwrap` and `expect` sits (test module or
  production); where `lumen-media::tempo` reads files (the load worker); how a
  warped clip reaches the engine; the format version, the migration and the
  fixtures; the E2E coverage of each slice.
- **The combined walkthrough** is the new E2E
  `walkthrough_warp::a_loop_at_another_tempo_is_in_time_one_hit_is_fixed_a_phrase_is_transposed_and_the_export_is_what_was_heard`.
  It is driven by dropped files, keys and the editor's own gestures:
  1. a loop named "92 BPM" (a click on each beat, one 80 ms late) and a steady
     tone are dropped into a 120 BPM song, and the loop follows the song with
     nothing done to it (Full mix at 92, eight beats long);
  2. played, the hits are 0.5 s apart and the late one is heard late;
  3. anchors on the hits either side and a drag of the late hit onto its beat
     put it on the grid within 10 ms and leave the anchors where they were;
  4. the tone is warped, set to Melodic and transposed +2 st from the Pitch chip;
  5. both clips are trimmed, faded and slipped in the Clip tab (the slip on the
     loop is undone, so its hits stay on the beat);
  6. played again, every hit is 0.5 s ± 25 ms apart;
  7. the song is exported, and the file has the same hits at the same
     distances (± 2 ms) as the test device played, with the tone at 415 to
     445 Hz (382 Hz × 2^(2/12) = 429 Hz).

  The test device drops up to a block of what it played before `listen`
  begins, so the first hit heard may be only its tail (the heard hits after it
  are identical to the export's, 640 frames earlier). The comparison starts at
  the second hit and says why. What the clips sound like is for MT-M9.5-1.

## Findings

No High findings.

### Medium

- **M1. `app/audio_editor/warp_markers.rs` was added at 941 lines of production
  code**, past the skill's split threshold of about 800. It holds the marker
  gestures, the keyboard moves, the ghost, the snapping and the drawing
  together. `lanes.rs`, which M9.5 touched, is at 1,274 production lines (it
  was already over and is not in the M9 report). Split `warp_markers.rs` by
  concern (gestures, keyboard, drawing) before later slices grow it; `lanes.rs`
  is older and is for the owner to schedule.
- **M2. One warp marker moves the audio on both sides of it, and nothing says
  so.** Dragging the late hit onto its beat with no other marker makes the hits
  after it play 61 ms early, because the audio after the last marker keeps its
  speed from that marker on. The exit criterion says "fix one late hit with a
  warp marker"; to do it a person has to know to anchor the hits either side
  first, and nothing in the strip, the tooltip or the guide tells them. The walkthrough
  anchors first, and `a_lone_marker_moves_the_hits_after_it_so_the_walkthrough_anchors_first`
  records the behaviour. **A decision, not a defect:** options are (A) leave it
  and teach it in the milestone's docs pass, (B) pin the neighbouring transients
  when a marker is made by a drag, with a modifier to turn it off, or (C) offer
  "Fix this hit" in the strip's context menu, which makes the three markers in
  one undo step. Recommendation: C, because it keeps the model as it is and
  puts the right action where the late hit is.
- **M3. Two gates could not run here** (WSL perf and clippy, macOS clippy),
  see Gates. Neither is a finding in the code. The performance gate has to be
  run in WSL before the milestone is signed off, since `lumen-project` changed.

- **M4. The plan's real-time test for warp was not written.** `docs/milestones/M9.5.md`
  §5 asks for "a counting-allocator test on the audio thread while warped
  clips play and markers are dragged". No test under the allocation guard
  mentions warp or stretch. The argument that it is covered: a warped clip is a
  `StreamAudio` at the callback, the guard test of streamed clips passes, and a
  marker drag sends a new clip list as every edit does. That is an argument,
  not a test, and it is the first rule. The test belongs in `lumen-app`
  (`live.rs` already installs the guard), since `lumen-engine` cannot see
  `media::warped`: play a warped clip through the device callback while a
  marker is dragged and a transpose is changed, and count violations.

### Low

- **L1. `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` still listed "Audio clips do not follow tempo"**,
  which M9.5 closes. Fixed on this branch: the row is removed. The two other
  rows the plan named were already gone.
- **L2. The documentation pass is owed.** Eleven ids are tagged `@m9.5` in
  `site/docs/data/undocumented.txt` (ADR-0133). The pass writes the guides,
  runs a full render, and adds `m9.5` to `docs_pass.txt`.
