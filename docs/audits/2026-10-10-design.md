# Interface audit after M4 (2026-10-10)

**Scope:** theming, type, spacing and sizing, UX, and motion across
`crates/lumen-app/src`, against `docs/DESIGN.md` and `CLAUDE.md` §5.
**Asked by:** the owner, after merging the M4 review fixes (PR #3).
**Evidence:** screenshots of `main` at `bf5ee76` (debug build, test device)
in dark and light at 100%, 150% and 200%, the shots harness, and a read of
the UI code. The mock-ups of every variant are an artifact:
https://claude.ai/artifact/MMfhW9cV9X463RvFYx9D7r (private to the owner).
**Outcome:** every proposal approved; built as milestone 4.5
(`docs/milestones/M4.5.md`). The owner's choices are in the
owner-decisions table ("Interface audit").

Tokens are clean: no colour, font size, radius or duration is written
inline outside `theme.rs`, `fonts.rs` and `motion.rs`. The findings are
tokens used on the wrong surface, gaps in the size scale, stock egui
widgets beside Lumen's own, and motion DESIGN.md promises but the app
lacks.
