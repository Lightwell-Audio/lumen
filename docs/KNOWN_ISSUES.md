# Lumen — Known issues

Problems found and not fixed yet, with what they affect and where they are
addressed. Fixed issues move to the relevant decision record or manual test
run. Add new ones here as they are found.

| Found | Issue | Effect | Addressed in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-10 (macOS CI) | **Space and Shift+Space toggle by what the audio thread last reported.** `Transport::play_stop` and `pause_continue` read the engine's transport state, which changes at the next audio block, so a second press before that block repeats the first command (Stop, Stop) instead of toggling back. Found as an E2E flake on a loaded macOS runner; the tests now wait for each change to be heard. | Two presses within one buffer (about 5 ms at 256 frames, 46 ms at 2048) leave playback stopped or playing; rare by hand | Toggle from the last command sent until the engine confirms it, with an E2E test; to pitch to the owner (it changes how Space acts) |
| 2026-10-07 (M3 2) | **Audio clips do not follow tempo.** Changing the tempo keeps each clip's start in beats; its audio plays at its own speed, so playback continues from the same bar at a different point in the file, and a clip's region can cut its audio short. | Expected until warping | Warp (S, later). Clip trimming (M3 6c) shipped and does not change how a clip follows tempo |
| 2026-10-08 (MT-APP-14 run 3) | **Files dragged in on macOS and Linux may not follow the pointer.** winit 0.30 gives no position while files are dragged over the window, and Lumen reads the system's cursor only on Windows (ADR-0032), so elsewhere a drop lands where egui last saw the pointer, or on new tracks at the end. | Dropping on a chosen track or between tracks may not work there; drops still import | winit 0.31 (drag positions on every system), or a cursor read per system, before the macOS and Linux builds are tested by hand |
| 2026-10-08 (M3 review L1) | **The Keyboard shortcuts window is cramped at 200% in the smallest window** (960 × 540 points): about two rows of the list show under the keyboard map. It works and scrolls. | Hard to browse shortcuts at large scales on a small screen | M10 polish, with "tall windows at large scales" (M3.md): a keyboard map that collapses, or shrinks when the window is short |
| 2026-10-09 (M4 slice 4b) | **Returns are not dragged on their headers** in the pinned area (they are reordered by dragging their mixer strips since slice 5, B8). Returns also show no sends of their own (sends between returns). | Reorder returns in the mixer | M10 polish: header drag for returns; sends between returns when a need shows |
| 2026-10-09 (M4 slice 4d) | **Browser audition does not play through the cue yet**: there is no browser audition (the plan has it join the cue). | Nothing to hear yet | With the browser's audition |
| 2026-10-09 (M4 slice 4d) | **Cue was not tested by hand**: the owner's interface has outputs 1/2 only. E2E checks it on the four-output test device. | Cue on real hardware unconfirmed | MT-MIX-4 step 9 on a four-output interface, when one is available |
| 2026-10-07 (M3 4c) | **Waveform peaks are not reclaimed** after a device-rate change until Lumen restarts (ADR-0027). | The atlas holds about 130 five-minute stereo files | Streaming (M9) |
| 2026-10-10 (M4 slice 6) | **Scrubbing while delay compensation is active is heard late, and loop-scrub restarts are not enveloped in time.** The scrubber renders through the mixer's delay lines, so what it plays comes out the compensation later, and its own 2 ms envelope falls before the restart it should hide (ADR-0041). | No effect until a device adds latency (only the synthetic test device can, `--test-latency`). With latency: scrub lags the pointer by the compensation and loop scrubs may click | M6, when devices bring latency: scrub through a path that skips the compensation, or blend at the tracks' inputs as jumps do |
| 2026-10-10 (M4 slice 6) | **A crowded transport bar shortens the audio status hard.** At 150% in a 1,600-pixel-wide window (about 1,070 pt) the device line shows a few letters, and a compensation readout only its figure ("Fa… · 12.4 ms"). The device details give way first, so the compensation keeps what room there is. | The full text stays in the tooltip, the info line and the audio panel | The transport bar's narrow layout (M10 accessibility and scaling pass), or a compact status when the bar is short |
| 2026-10-10 (CI fixes) | **Waveforms are unverified on OpenGL.** On Mesa's llvmpipe (OpenGL), the atlas's negative peaks read back as 0. A read-back cannot tell whether the upload or the read-back loses them. Lumen may fall back to OpenGL where there is no Vulkan, DX12 or Metal. The atlas test now runs on Vulkan, DX12 or Metal only (lavapipe on the Linux CI). | Waveforms may show only their upper half on an OpenGL-only machine | Draw a waveform on OpenGL and look, before the Linux build is tested by hand |
| 2026-10-10 (CI fixes) | **E2E `mixing::mixer_changes_while_playing_never_click` failed once under a 2× CPU overload** (eight busy loops on four cores, the whole suite in parallel). It heard a step above the click limit. It passed four times in a row under the same load when run alone, and has not failed in CI. | Possibly a click when the machine is starved, or a test artefact | Look at the glide's timing under load if it shows again in CI |
